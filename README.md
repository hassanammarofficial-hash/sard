# Sard | سَرد

**Graduation Project | Rwaad Egypt**

## 📌 Project Overview

Sard is a digital platform designed to simplify the discovery of Arabic knowledge content, helping users find and choose relevant books, podcasts, and other educational content based on their interests and needs.

The project addresses the challenge of content overload and fragmentation across different platforms, where users often struggle to identify what content is worth their time.

Sard focuses on creating a more guided discovery experience by helping users:

- 🔎 Discover relevant Arabic knowledge content
- 🎯 Find content that matches their interests and needs
- ⚖️ Compare available content and make informed choices
- 💡 Move from simply consuming content toward reflection and practical application

### Core Experience

**Discover → Listen / Read → Save → Reflect → Apply**

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- 🔎 Simplify the discovery of relevant Arabic knowledge content.
- 🎯 Help users find content that matches their interests and needs.
- ⚖️ Make it easier for users to compare different content platforms and available options.
- ⏱️ Reduce the time and effort users spend searching for suitable content.
- 💡 Create a more meaningful content experience that goes beyond simply listening or reading.
- 🚀 Bridge the gap between consuming knowledge and turning it into reflection and practical application.
